Privacy

Lumid HQ by Lumid · Last updated September 2026

Your data stays on your device

Everything you add to Lumid HQ — ideas, tasks, notes, plans, contacts and saved links — is stored in your browser on this device. We don't run servers that receive or store your content, and we don't sell or share your data.

Backups

Because data lives in your browser, clearing site data or switching browsers removes it. Use Settings → Export JSON to keep a backup and Import to restore it on another device.

Services the app contacts

  • GitHub Pages hosts the app files.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces.
  • noembed.com is asked for the title of links you save to Resources, and YouTube serves video thumbnails.
  • Voice capture uses your browser's built-in speech recognition, which may be processed by your browser vendor (for example Google or Apple).

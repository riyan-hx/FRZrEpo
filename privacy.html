Privacy

The Forge by Lumid · Last updated September 2026

The short version

Your notes are yours. Without an account, everything stays in your browser on your device. With an account, we store a copy so it can sync between your devices. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

What we store

  • On your device: everything you add — ideas, tasks, notes, plans, contacts and saved links — in your browser's storage.
  • If you create an account: your email address, a synced copy of your data, and your plan status (trial or subscription). This is stored with Supabase, our database provider.
  • If you subscribe: payment is handled by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. We never see or store your card details; we receive your subscription status and a link to manage billing.

Services we use

  • Supabase — accounts and synced data.
  • Lemon Squeezy — checkout, billing, invoices and sales tax.
  • Google — if you choose "Continue with Google", and for fonts.
  • Cloudflare — hosting and privacy-friendly page analytics (no cookies, no personal profiles).
  • Sentry — crash reports to fix bugs (no note contents).
  • noembed.com and YouTube — titles and thumbnails for links you save.
  • Voice capture uses your browser's built-in speech recognition, which may be processed by your browser vendor.

Your rights

  • Export: Settings → Export JSON downloads everything at any time.
  • Delete: Settings → Account → Delete account permanently removes your account and synced data, and cancels any subscription. Data on your device stays until you clear it.
  • You can also ask us to access, correct or delete your data by email.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Database rules ensure each account can only read and write its own data.

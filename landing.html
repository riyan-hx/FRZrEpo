FAQ

Questions

Do I need a card for the trial?

No. Create an account and you get 30 days of Pro. If you don't subscribe, nothing is charged — syncing just pauses and your data stays on your device.

What is the founding member price?

The first 100 people to subscribe pay $3/month instead of $5 — and keep that price for as long as they stay subscribed. It's applied automatically at checkout.

Is it an app I install?

It runs in your browser and installs like an app: on iPhone, Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen; on Android, Chrome → Install app.

Who can see my notes?

Only you. Without an account nothing leaves your device. With an account, database rules ensure each account can only read its own data.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Cancel any time from Manage billing. New subscriptions have a 14-day no-questions refund — see the refund policy.

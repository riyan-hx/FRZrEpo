Try before you pay

Every account starts with a free Pro trial, no card needed, so you can decide whether Lumid HQ is right for you before paying.

14-day money-back guarantee

If you're not happy with a new subscription, email us within 14 days of the payment and we'll refund it in full — no questions asked. This applies to your first payment and to yearly renewals.

Cancelling

Cancel any time from Settings → Account → Manage billing. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for, and you won't be charged again. Monthly renewals after the first month aren't refunded.

How refunds are paid

Refunds are processed by Lemon Squeezy to your original payment method and usually arrive within 5–10 business days.
